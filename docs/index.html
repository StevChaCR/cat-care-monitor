Open local app ↗

One small setup. Then, your browser.

  1. Download and extract the Cat Care Monitor project.
  2. On Windows, double-click start_web_windows.bat. Python 3.13 is required.
  3. Keep its terminal open. It opens the app in your browser automatically.

GitHub Pages hosts this interface. The local engine connects to the camera and runs Python. The public website does not collect camera credentials or photos.

BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Better learning.

    Different days and moments
    At least 3 independent recording sessions, with labeled cats in each split.
  • 02
    A few different points of view
    Include walking, resting, hidden faces, daytime, and infrared scenes.
  • 03
    Carefully reviewed boxes
    Label every visible cat. Confirm empty scenes too. Unreviewed images block training.
  • 04
    Room for an honest check
    Whole sessions are held out for validation and testing. A new model is activated only when you choose it.
