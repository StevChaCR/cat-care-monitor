Collect station clips
Developer only

            
Use fixed food and water zones that include the cat's head and the bowl. Capture both real feeding and look-alikes: sniffing, sitting, grooming, and walking past.

            
            

            
            
            
Each clip contains 16 cropped frames over about 4 seconds. Collection includes empty scenes and missed detections. Up to 120 clips per camera run; 600 active clips per profile.
Choose a station, then start collection when the moment is useful. Stop collecting before changing the area. A water clip is not automatically labeled Drinking; review the actual activity.

    What are recording periods?
    A period is one genuine recording occasion, such as Monday morning, Tuesday evening, or Wednesday morning. Different occasions on the same day can be separate; three days are not mandatory. Keep nearby frames and simultaneous food/water clips together. A new name or a reconnection does not make the same footage independent.
    This program keeps at least three usable periods to separate training, validation, and test. Whole periods are assigned to one set; class coverage is checked in every set. These minimums do not establish broad accuracy.
